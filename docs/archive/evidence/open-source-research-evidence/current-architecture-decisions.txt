Protected architecture facts
----------------------------
- M0 remains As-Built and separate from planned target architecture.
- RECA remains a modular monolith, not a microservice system.
- The sole Celery App is app.core.celery:celery_app.
- GROBID and MinIO are health/smoke foundations, not completed business flows.
- Third-party objects must not become RECA core domain objects.
- Direct integration may be used when it is cheaper and preserves boundaries.
- Adapter use is required when replacement, offline mocking, object isolation,
  licensing, security or testability justifies it; it is not globally mandatory.
- Business writes remain behind Service, authorization, version and approval rules.
- One orchestrator Agent remains scheduled for M8; no free multi-Agent system.
- EvidenceSpan, deterministic results and version lineage remain RECA-owned truth.
